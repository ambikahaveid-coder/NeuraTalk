# NeuraTalk Legal Framework - Production Ready

## 1. Terms of Service (ToS)
- **AI Limitation Clause**: NeuraTalk uses AI for real-time translation. Accuracy is not 100% guaranteed. Users accept that nuances or dialects may be misinterpreted.
- **Voice Cloning Consent**: By using the "Voice Cloning" feature, users grant NeuraTalk a temporary license to process their voice for communication purposes only. Data is not stored for model training without explicit opt-in.
- **Telecom Compliance**: NeuraTalk acts as a bridge. Users are responsible for complying with local laws regarding recording and intercepting communications.

## 2. Privacy Policy (GDPR & DPDP Act Compliant)
- **Data Minimization**: We collect only what is necessary (Phone number for OTP, Audio stream for live translation).
- **Retention Policy**: Audio data is deleted immediately after the call ends, unless recording is explicitly enabled by both parties (B2B/B2C requirement).
- **Regional Storage**: Indian user data remains within sovereign borders as per the DPDP Act 2023.

## 3. Recording Consent Policy
- NeuraTalk enforces a "Dual-Party Consent" model. If recording is active, a system-generated voice in the target language will notify all participants.
