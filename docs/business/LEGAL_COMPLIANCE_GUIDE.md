- If B2B customers (companies) process personal data using your service

- Must be in place BEFORE B2B customer signs up

### Template Key Sections

```markdown
# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (DPA)

## 1. DEFINITIONS
- "Personal Data": Any information relating to identified/identifiable person

- "Controller": Customer (the company)
- "Processor": NeuraChat (service provider)

## 2. SCOPE
This DPA applies to NeuraChat processing personal data on behalf of
Customer.

## 3. SUBJECT MATTER & DURATION
- Subject Matter: Provision of NeuraChat Service
- Duration: [X] months or until service ends

## 4. TYPES OF DATA & CATEGORIES
- Employee names, phone, email
- Customer phone, email 
- Call metadata (date, time, duration)

## 5. CATEGORIES OF DATA SUBJECTS
- Company employees
- Customers
- Contacts

## 6. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING
- Provide Service
- Billing & payment

- Prevent fraud

## 7. OBLIGATIONS OF PROCESSOR (NeuraChat)
We will:
- Process data ONLY per Customer instructions
- Ensure authorized persons sign confidentiality agreements

## 11. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
```

## 🎙️ STEP 4: RECORDING CONSENT FORM

**CRITICAL**: Laws differ by jurisdiction!

### US Jurisdictions:

**1-Party Consent States** (35 states):
```
Only 1 party needs to consent to recording. You can record if:
- You are a participant in the call
- You notify the other party at start of call
- Sign: "By continuing, you consent to recording"

States: [List all applicable]
```

**2-Party Consent States** (15 states + DC):
```
Both parties MUST consent to recording. You must:
- Get explicit agreement BEFORE recording
- Show checkbox: "I consent to call being recorded"
- Both parties check box
- Cannot record if either party refuses
- Penalty: Criminal liability + civil damages

States: CA, FL, IL, PA, TX, VA,WA, etc.
```

### Sample Recording Consent Form

```markdown
# CALL RECORDING CONSENT

This call [WILL / MAY] be recorded for:
- Quality assurance
- Staff training  
- Legal protection
- Customer service improvement

The recording will be:
- Stored for 30 days, then deleted
- Kept confidential
- Used only by NeuraChat staff
- Not shared with third parties

YOUR CONSENT:
☐ I consent to this call being recorded
☐ I DO NOT consent (recording will not be made)

☐ I understand the uses of the recording
☐ I understand I can request deletion at any time

By continuing with this call, you consent to recording.

[Accept] [Decline]
```

### Implementation in App

```javascript
// Before call connects
show_recording_consent_modal = () => {

    user_id,

  })
}
```
